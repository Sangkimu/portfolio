Frequently asked questions

How is the final price confirmed?

After reviewing your brief, the required deliverables, complexity and timeline, a written quotation is prepared for your approval before work starts.

Can I request a service that is not listed?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe your requirements. Custom requests are assessed individually.

What counts as a revision?

A revision is a reasonable adjustment to an agreed direction or draft. A new concept, major change of brief or additional deliverable may require a revised quotation.

Do package prices include printing or advertising?

No, not by default. Printing, advertising spend, paid stock assets, hosting and other third-party costs are separate unless listed in the approved quotation.
